## Attempt 2 — OpenCode upstream loader (7b3d4ce3, v2 dev)

  The loader is TypeScript (Effect + jsonc-parser + immer). Running it needs the repo's dependency
  graph (bun/node_modules). bun is ABSENT, the deps are not installed, and installing them requires a
  full package fetch. Not exercised here.

  Note on the "run the shipped client" shortcut: it is explicitly forbidden by the ConfigLens spec
  (no client binary execution, even `--version`) and by this task (no agents/plugins/network model
  calls). `@openai/codex` and `@opencode/cli` ship wrapper binaries; invoking them would violate that
  rule, so it was not attempted.
